📢 About the position

As a Tech Lead at Zylinc, you will play a pivotal role in shaping our technology
stack and architectural decisions. You will be embedded in Team Warrior, a
cross-functional product team consisting of a product manager, a product designer,
and seven engineers.

We operate with a modern product model inspired by Marty Cagan, giving product
teams end-to-end ownership of solving customer problems in ways they love while
still working for our business. As Tech Lead, you'll hold overall responsibility
for the technical feasibility of the solutions we build.

You'll spend approximately 50% of your time writing code, while the remaining time
is dedicated to researching and evaluating technology choices, supporting product
discovery with technical insights, refining user stories and specifications, and
mentoring colleagues through code reviews and pair programming.

You will also be part of our Architecture Team, collaborating with fellow tech leads
to ensure our choices align with our long-term product vision and business goals.

📝 What you'll do

- **Build and enhance features** together with your team, ensuring high-quality code and smooth user experiences across our products.
- **Lead the technical direction**, from system design to project execution, ensuring alignment with business goals and long-term scalability.
- **Collaborate with stakeholders** across engineering, product, and design to clarify requirements and refine implementation details.
- **Design scalable and maintainable architecture**, advocating for patterns and structures that reduce tech debt and enable flexibility.
- **Evaluate and select tools, frameworks, and technologies**, based on technical merit and team needs - not hype.
- **Mentor and support engineers**, fostering a learning culture through code reviews, architectural guidance, and collaborative problem-solving.
- **Drive our use of AI**, both to improve engineering workflows and to explore opportunities for integrating AI features in our products.
- **Balance architecture and delivery**, helping the team find pragmatic paths forward without compromising code quality.
- **Lead by example**, writing production-grade code, participating actively in code reviews, and troubleshooting complex issues with your team.

🏆 What you'll bring

- You are fluent in **.NET and C#**, with a solid understanding of the ecosystem and real-world experience building business applications.
- You bring a **backend mindset** but understand the frontend context enough to help others improve their implementation, avoid pitfalls, and ensure a cohesive end-to-end experience
- You're a practical user of **AI-assisted coding tools**, and see them as a natural extension of your productivity and quality toolkit.
- You have structured backend experience, and know how to organize codebases for readability, testing, and long-term maintenance.
- You've worked with **Entity Framework**, **RESTful APIs**, and **SignalR**, or equivalent technologies in similar domains.
- You take a **user-focused approach** to feature development-seeking to understand the "why" behind requirements to build things that actually make a difference.
- You contribute actively to technical discussions, advocate for your ideas with clarity, and are open to finding common ground with the team.
- You are proficient in **English**, both written and spoken, and comfortable working in a setting where clear communication matters.

❤️ Bonus points if you have

- Experience working with **React**, **TypeScript**, or modern frontend tooling.
- Familiarity with **Azure**, especially in deploying or managing cloud-native services.
- Hands-on involvement with **DevOps** practices, including **CI/CD** pipelines and **Kubernetes**.
- Knowledge or experience with **Test-Driven Development (TDD)**, and a good sense of when to apply it.
- You are proficient in **Danish**, as it can be helpful for communication with colleagues.
